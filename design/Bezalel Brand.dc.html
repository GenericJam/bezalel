Self-review · Recommendation
The strongest is 1a Lamp: wordmark in Garamond, Hebrew name beneath, set on tekhelet leather.
Why 1a winsIt holds at 20pt because it's two solid masses with no strokes to fill in. It's the reference readers recognize fastest. And it belongs to Bezalel himself, who made the lamps. It also gives night reading its own symbol, and the gold-on-blue icon is easy to spot on a home screen.
Where the others fall short1b is the cleverest idea, but the book blurs below 40pt and the sword can feel combative. 1c fits the product best, but it reads as a notes app first. The almond nod is too subtle to carry the brand.
Risk + mitigationLamps and flames are common church marks. The shallow bowl and the cut-out inner flame set this one apart, so keep both. You could reuse the 1b blade as the cross-reference icon and the 1c nib as the build-a-tool icon inside the app.
